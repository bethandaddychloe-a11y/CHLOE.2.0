Upcoming news

High and medium impact news for USD and JPY. Tap Analyse and Chloe 2 researches the event by itself, then gives BUY, SELL or WAIT for each pair. Quick mode takes a few seconds; Deep mode runs four research agents on the web and is slower. Every analysis you run before a release is saved to your Scorecard and checked automatically afterwards.

The odds are an AI estimate from web research, not a data feed. Prices spike both ways in the first minutes after a release, so read the call as the lean for the move after the spike. The calendar comes from a free weekly feed, so check the forecast before you trade it.
